Questions

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No. The 14-day Pro trial starts without payment details. After the trial you can stay on the Free plan or choose a paid plan.

Can I use TestFlow for automated tests?

Yes. Manual testers record results in test runs, and on the Pro plan you can import results from Selenium, Playwright or Cypress.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time, and your data stays available on the Free plan.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each company's workspace is kept separate.
